Questions? Answers.

KITE H7 works with custom firmware stacks, including ArduPilot and PX4. It ships with firmware pre‑installed and can be reflashed over USB or SD card.

KITE H7 has two independent IMUs, the ICM‑42688 (TDK InvenSense) and the BMI088 (Bosch). The redundant setup improves vibration resistance and attitude estimation, and keeps performance reliable during aggressive manoeuvres.

The board provides 8 UARTs, 14 PWM outputs, I²C, SPI and CAN bus. It has 2 GPS ports and 3 telemetry ports, all on JST‑GH connectors for reliable, compact wiring.

The KITE H7 main board with pre‑installed firmware, JST‑GH connectors and power cables, and a quick start guide with technical manual.

KITE H7 is rated from −40 °C to +85 °C, so it suits missions in extreme cold and high heat.
